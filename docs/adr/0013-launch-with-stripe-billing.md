# Launch with Stripe billing

The rebuilt AgentUtils product will launch with Stripe subscription billing rather than remaining free throughout beta. It will offer Free, Plus at US$19 per month and Pro at US$49 per month, using fixed resource limits without overage charges or an Enterprise plan. Checkout, subscription lifecycle webhooks and customer billing management are part of the rebuild; limits will measure Connections, Inboxes, Events, retention and File storage rather than generic API calls.
